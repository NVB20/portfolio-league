# Future features

Ideas we would build after the assignment. None of them exist yet.

## Social

- **Friends and private leagues.** Players add friends and create their own leagues.
- **Public or private trades.** Each player chooses whether others can see their trades.

## Real-market leagues

- **Live contests.** Contests that follow the real market's open and close, not the game's price history.
- **Real accounts, read only.** Players connect Bybit or Binance with a read-only key, and the app shows their real trading results.
- **Later: trade through IBKR.** Players connect the IBKR gateway and trade real money from the app. Players are ranked by % gain, not by money, so a small account can beat a big one.

## Trading journal with AI analysis

- **First version:** each trade is shown next to the events around it: earnings reports, dividends and big news (elections, wars).
- **Later:** analysis that fits each player's trading style.

## Bringing players back

One rule for everything in this section: only trading changes cash or holdings. These features reward players with status or information, never with money.

### Limited clock jumps

Each player gets 5 clock jumps per hour. When they run out, the player has to come back later to keep playing.

Today there is one clock for everyone, and only admins can jump it (D-017). This idea needs a separate clock for each player, for example in a solo practice mode.

### Notifications

- **Quiet on weekends.** No notifications on Friday and Saturday.
- **Each player chooses which notifications they get:**

| Notification | Example |
|--------------|---------|
| A stock you follow | "Nvidia is down 4% today" |
| A stock you hold | "Teva is up 6% and you hold it" |
| Market opening | "TASE opens in 15 minutes" |
| Your place | "You dropped to 7th" |
| Your orders | "Your order filled at the open" |
| Your streak | "One task left to keep your 12-day streak" |

### Daily challenge and streak

Like Duolingo: one small task a day. For example:

- Spend 10 minutes in the app.
- Open 1 new position.

The streak itself is built (D-039): today it counts any real Israel day the player is in the app. A daily task would make finishing it what keeps the streak going. If the task isn't done yet, a notification reminds the player before the real Israel day ends.

### Badges

Badges are only for show. They never give cash.

- First US stock
- Beat the TA-35 this week
- 10, 100 and 1,000 trades
- A week up 5%, 10%, 15% or 20%

### Weekly review

Once a week, each player sees:

- Their return against the TA-35, the S&P 500 and the Nasdaq-100, and their alpha (how far they beat them).
- Their place compared with last week.
